01 · Concept

THE IDEA

Traditional retail promotions say: "Spend X, get a pressure cooker." This creates transactional value but very little emotional memory. Modern Saree Palace offers a different path: Spend ₹10,000 and receive something you actually want for your lifestyle.

THE ₹10K EFFECT

₹7K ₹8K ₹9K ₹10K

Designed to encourage higher-value baskets. A customer already shopping around ₹7,000–₹9,000 has a clear psychological reason to complete the purchase and unlock a premium reward.

আর একটু কিনলেই প্রিমিয়াম উপহার — এই সহজ লক্ষ্যটাই কেনাকাটাকে এগিয়ে নিতে পারে।
